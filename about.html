About the business

Built from real wedding experience and genuine care.

After years behind the camera, Kara saw firsthand how much a calm and organised presence changes the entire wedding experience. KLBK combines photography, videography, content creation, and planning support to create a wedding experience that feels seamless, supportive, and genuinely personal.

The approach is relationship-focused, clear, and warm. Couples are guided with communication that feels steady rather than overwhelming, so they can enjoy the day instead of managing every detail alone.

Founder

Hi, I'm Kara, the heart behind Kapturing Love by K.

I'm passionate about capturing love in its most genuine form, preserving the moments that often go unnoticed but mean the most.

For me, it's not just about photos or films. It's about creating something you can feel, something that takes you straight back to your day, exactly as it was.

I work with couples across NSW who want to feel genuinely supported — not just on their wedding day, but throughout the whole process. If you want someone in your corner who cares as much as you do, I would love to chat.
