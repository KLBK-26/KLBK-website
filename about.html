About the business

Built from real wedding experience and genuine care.

After years behind the camera, Kara saw firsthand how much a calm and organised presence changes the entire wedding experience. KLBK combines photography, videography, content creation, and planning support to create a wedding experience that feels seamless, supportive, and genuinely personal.

The approach is relationship-focused, clear, and warm. Couples are guided with communication that feels steady rather than overwhelming, so they can enjoy the day instead of managing every detail alone.

— Maddie & Sarah
