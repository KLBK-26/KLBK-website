Why an Engagement Session is Worth It Before Your Wedding

An engagement session might feel like an optional extra — a nice idea but not essential. Here's why most couples who do one are glad they did.

You get comfortable with your photographer

The biggest benefit of an engagement session isn't the photos. It's the time you spend with your photographer before the wedding.

By the time your wedding day arrives, you already know how they give direction, what feels natural, and how to move without overthinking it. That comfort shows in the photos.

You get images you actually use

Engagement photos are perfect for save-the-dates, wedding websites, and social media. Many couples also use them for signage or table displays at the reception.

It's lower stakes than the wedding day

Your engagement session is relaxed, unhurried, and just the two of you. There's no timeline pressure, no guests watching, no ceremony to get to. It's a good space to figure out what works — and what doesn't — before it actually matters.
