Wedding Photography

What's Included in a Wedding Photography Package?

Wedding photography packages can be confusing. Coverage hours, edited images, turnaround times, add-ons — it's a lot to compare across different photographers.

Here's a straightforward breakdown of what to look for.

Coverage hours

Most wedding photography packages are built around hours of coverage. Four hours suits an elopement or micro wedding. Six to eight hours covers most standard weddings. Full day coverage suits larger celebrations with a long run sheet.

Think about when you want coverage to start — getting ready shots require time before the ceremony — and when you're happy to wrap up.

Edited images

The number of edited images varies depending on your photographer and coverage length. Most full-day weddings result in several hundred edited images. Ask your photographer what the typical range looks like for your package.

Gallery delivery

Find out how and when your photos are delivered. At Kapturing Love by K, edited galleries are delivered through a secure online gallery within four to six weeks of your wedding day.

What's not included by default

Second photographers, engagement sessions, wedding albums, and travel fees are often available as add-ons rather than standard inclusions. Ask about these upfront so there are no surprises.
