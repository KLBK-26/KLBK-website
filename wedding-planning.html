FAQ

A few helpful notes before we chat.

What is Day-of Coordination?

Wedding day management with thoughtful preparation beforehand. KLBK reviews your plans, confirms key details, communicates with suppliers, supports setup, and keeps the day moving calmly.

When should we book a coordinator?

As soon as you know you want support. Many couples book months ahead to protect their date, especially for 2026 and 2027 weddings.

Do you only help on the wedding day?

No. Even day-of coordination includes planning preparation. Partial and full planning support can begin much earlier.

Can we book planning and photography or videography together?

Yes. The consultation can explore how services work together comfortably.

Do you travel?

Yes. KLBK supports weddings across NSW including Tamworth, Hunter Valley, Sydney, Central Coast, Newcastle, and regional areas.
